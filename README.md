#  AI Workforce Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **AI Workforce Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Tableau** to analyze employee productivity, burnout, job satisfaction, salary, remote work, education, and AI adoption.

##  Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze workforce-related data and identify patterns in:

* Employee productivity
* Employee burnout
* Job satisfaction
* AI adoption
* Remote work
* Employee education
* Salary across industries
* Attrition risk
* Job satisfaction by country

##  Key KPIs

| KPI                      |    Value |
| ------------------------ | -------: |
| Total Employees          |    1,500 |
| Average Burnout Score    |    50.06 |
| Average Job Satisfaction |    3.335 |
| Average Employee Salary  | $140.73K |
| AI Adoption Rate         |    59.5% |

##  Dashboard Visualizations

### Productivity vs Burnout

A scatter plot showing the relationship between employee productivity and burnout, with salary represented through bubble size and attrition risk through color.

### Remote Work Distribution

A donut chart showing the distribution of employees working:

* Fully Remote
* Hybrid
* On-site

### AI Adoption Stage

Shows employees across different AI adoption stages:

* AI-First
* Experimenting
* Optimizing
* Integrating

### Education Level Distribution

Compares the number of employees across different education levels.

### Job Satisfaction by Country

A geographical visualization showing employee job satisfaction across countries.

### Average Salary by Industry

Compares average employee salary across different industries.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

* Industry
* Country
* Remote Work Type
* Education Level
* Attrition Risk

These filters allow users to interactively explore different segments of the workforce.

## Key Insights

The dashboard helps understand how factors such as productivity, burnout, AI adoption, remote work, education, and industry are related to the modern workforce.
